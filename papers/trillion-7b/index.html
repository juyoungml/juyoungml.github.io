Trillion 7B Technical Report

Sungjun Han, Juyoung Suk, Suyeong An, Hyungguk Kim, Kyuseok Kim, Wonsuk Yang, Seungtaek Choi, Jamin Shin

  • arXiv
  • PDF
  • Hugging Face

Abstract

We introduce Trillion-7B, the most token-efficient Korean-centric multilingual LLM available. Our novel Cross-lingual Document Attention (XLDA) mechanism enables highly efficient and effective knowledge transfer from English to target languages like Korean and Japanese. Combined with optimized data mixtures, language-specific filtering, and tailored tokenizer construction, Trillion-7B achieves competitive performance while dedicating only 10% of its 2T training tokens to multilingual data and requiring just 59.4K H100 GPU-hours ($148K) for full training.

Comprehensive evaluations across 27 benchmarks in four languages demonstrate Trillion-7B's robust multilingual performance and exceptional cross-lingual consistency.
